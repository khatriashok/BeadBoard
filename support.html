Support

How to play, common questions, and how to reach us

How to play

  • Magnet pen: tap or drag across the holes to pull beads up.
  • Push down: tap or drag over beads to drop them back into their holes.
  • Shake clear: shakes the board and drops every bead.
  • Pattern cards: pick a card and dashed rings show where the beads go. Fill exactly those holes and you'll hear a chime.
  • Colors: tap a colored dot to change the board color.
  • Sound: the button at the top turns sound on or off.

Common questions

I can't hear any sound

Check that the button at the top says Sound on and that the device volume is turned up. Sound starts after the first tap on the board.

Can I save a drawing?

Not yet. Like the real toy, the board starts fresh each time. Take a screenshot to keep a drawing.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes. On iPad the board fills the screen. Turn the iPad sideways and the board sits on the left with the tools and pattern cards on the right.

Does it need Wi-Fi or data?

No. Bead Board works fully offline, including on a plane.

Is it safe for my child?

Bead Board has no ads, no in-app purchases, no links out of the app and no chat, and it collects no personal information. See the privacy policy.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 17 or later.
