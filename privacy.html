Privacy Policy

Effective November 3, 2026

In short: Bead Board does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information.
  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No ads, analytics or tracking
  • No internet connection is used
  • No in-app purchases
  • No access to the camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location

What the app stores

Bead Board saves one setting on your device: whether sound is turned on or off. This stays on the device, is never sent anywhere, and is deleted when you delete the app.

Drawings on the board are not saved. They disappear when the app is closed.

What the app collects

Nothing. Bead Board does not collect personal information, device identifiers, usage data, crash reports or diagnostics. The app does not connect to the internet or to any server.

Third parties

Bead Board contains no third-party code for advertising, analytics or tracking, and no information is shared with anyone.

Children's privacy

Bead Board is made for children and families. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country). The app is designed with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws in mind. The app contains no links out of the app and no ways to contact other people.

Your choices

Because no personal data is collected, there is nothing to access, export or delete. To remove the saved sound setting, delete the app.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, analytics or tracking of its own. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles information, this page will be updated first and the effective date above will change.
