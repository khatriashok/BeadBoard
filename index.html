Bead Board

A magnetic bead drawing board for iPhone and iPad

Drag the magnet pen across the board to pull steel beads up. Push them back down, shake the board to clear it, or pick a pattern card and trace a heart, a house, a star or a letter.

It works fully offline, has no ads and no in-app purchases, and collects no information about you or your child.
